## Profile image

The Bone mark (`blazon-bone`) on a Graphite square. The mark is not placed on Signal Orange.

## Posts

Headlines in Big Shoulders Display (`type.h1` or `type.h2`) on Bone or Graphite. Body text in IBM Plex Sans (`type.body`). Signal Orange is used once per image, for the thing the post is about.

## Do not

- Add shadows or blur: the only shadow is the hard `shadow.plate`.
- Round the corners of an image or a card.

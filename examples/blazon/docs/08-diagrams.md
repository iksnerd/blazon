## Lines and boxes

Boxes and connectors use the 2px Graphite rule. Secondary relations use the 1px Steel hairline.

## Labels

Labels are set in IBM Plex Mono at `type.label`. Longer notes use IBM Plex Sans at `type.small`.

## Emphasis

Signal Orange marks the step or part that acts. At most one thing in a diagram is orange.

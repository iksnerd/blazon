`blazon logo --on` and `get_logo` now use a logo whose sidecar states an `ink` in place of a plain configured `onLight` or `onDark` file wherever that ink reaches the bar, the highest ratio winning, as the manual says. Before, the configured files competed first by ratio. Only when no inked logo reaches the bar are the configured files with no stated ink judged as black and white. The viewer measures a gradient with core's contrast function, and its copy is gone.

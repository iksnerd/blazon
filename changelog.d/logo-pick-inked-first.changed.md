`blazon logo --on` and `get_logo` now use a logo whose sidecar states an `ink` in place of a plain configured `onLight` or `onDark` file wherever that ink reaches the bar, the highest ratio winning, as the manual says. Where no inked logo reaches the bar, they pick the dark-surface variant wherever white reaches 3:1 (and its `logo.deny` and colorway allow it), else the light one, where 0.8.0 picked the higher ratio of the two. The viewer measures a gradient with core's contrast function, and its copy is gone.
